Our first result. On all ten trains, and in nine of ten leave-one-out folds, CELOE returns exactly this concept: there exists a wagon that is closed and short. That is the rule Michalski himself reported in 1977. Its length is five. Accuracy is 0.90 for the reference setting and 1.00 with the restricted language or EL. The decision trees get 0.40 to 0.50 and the forests 0.60 to 0.70. The tree's rule is about wagon slot numbers: it fits ten rows perfectly but means nothing. The one CELOE error is fold 8, which we come back to in a moment.
