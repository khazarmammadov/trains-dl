Decision tree and random forest on two flat encodings: per wagon slot, and per-train counts

Learned concepts are scored on held-out trains with our own closed-world evaluator, which reproduces DL-Learner’s training accuracies exactly.

Here is the experimental design. The reference setting is CELOE with the closed-world reasoner, the full ALC language with numbers, and five seconds. Each other setting changes one thing: the language, the search algorithm, the time budget, the noise tolerance, or the reasoner. That is 478 DL-Learner runs in total, all with their .conf files in the repository. DL-Learner only reports training accuracy, so we wrote a small evaluator that applies the learned concept to the held-out trains with the same closed-world semantics. The baselines use exactly the same folds, on two ways of flattening the trains: one column per wagon slot, or counts per attribute value.
