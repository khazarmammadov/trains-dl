Team 6 · Concept Induction on Michalski’s Trains

The second limit is the open-world assumption. DL-Learner's closed-world reasoner treats what is not stated as false, which is very fast: under a millisecond per query. With HermiT under the open-world assumption, queries take 14 milliseconds, and 212 with our closure axioms, so in five seconds CELOE only tests 167 or 14 concepts and returns nothing useful. With sixty seconds it recovers the same rule on both ontologies. That works because an existential concept means the same in both worlds. Universal and negated concepts, like "all wagons are closed or short", only become provable with the closure axioms, and those are exactly what makes reasoning slow.
