Team 6 · Concept Induction on Michalski’s Trains

To conclude. Concept induction gave us the relational bias for free: "some wagon is short and closed" is a single existential restriction, whatever the number or order of wagons. It recovers the true rule from nine examples, and its rules are short, exact and match what humans wrote. The baselines are fast and need no tuning, have no hard expressivity wall, and don't need to be told the noise level. The limits: Michie's official rule needs "the same load shape" on two wagons, a shared variable that ALC cannot express, so no setting finds it. Results depend on the bias we choose, and with ten or twenty-four trains each train is many accuracy points. Overall, symbolic learning works very well, but only with the right bias and a target that is expressible at all. Thank you, we are happy to take questions.
