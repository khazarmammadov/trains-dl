Team 6 · Concept Induction on Michalski’s Trains

The task: given a knowledge base and positive and negative examples, find a description-logic class expression that covers the positives and none of the negatives. Our data is Michalski's trains: ten trains, five going east and five going west. Each train pulls a variable number of wagons, and each wagon has its own attributes and loads, so it is genuinely relational. Ten examples cannot tell us whether a learner generalises, so we added Michie's 24-train challenge, which ships in the same UCI archive, and 200 synthetic trains labelled by a hidden rule, plus a copy with ten percent of labels flipped. We checked the parsing: Michalski's labels agree 10 out of 10 with the classic rule, and Michie's 24 out of 24 with his published official rule.
