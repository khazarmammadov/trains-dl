Concept Induction on Michalski’s Trains

What description-logic learning can and cannot do, compared with decision trees and random forests

Team 6 · Khazar Mammadov, Turan Abbasli

27 November 2026

Hello, we are Team 6. Our project applies concept induction in description logics, using DL-Learner, to the UCI Trains dataset, and compares it with classical machine learning. In about twelve minutes we cover the data, how we built the knowledge base, our experiments, and what we learned about the strengths and limits of symbolic learning.
